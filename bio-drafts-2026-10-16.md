# Yixin Cui — Website Bio Drafts

## 中文網站版

崔浥新（2002 年生）是現居紐約州羅切斯特的作曲家，創作涵蓋管弦樂、室內樂、聲樂與音樂劇場，亦延伸至結合中國手語與打擊樂的編舞。

語言遊戲是其創作的重要方法，透過借音、訓讀、擬古與語域切換，在字形、發音和意義之間製造錯位。[《罪狀體邪馬臺綺譚 其二》](https://yixincui.com/works/saijotai-yamatai-khitan-no-2/) 將 libido、jouissance 等現代理論詞彙的聲音嵌入擬古漢字與日語訓讀；在音樂劇場作品 [*Twink Death*](https://yixincui.com/works/twink-death/) 中，古代人物以網路口語和理論話語交談，傳統音樂與當代酷兒文化相互映照，涉及酷兒現象學、pornotopia 與 queertopia 等觀念。

管弦樂作品 [《Impasse of the Weasels 鼬ノ困》](https://yixincui.com/works/impasse-of-the-weasels/) 從東北旅行中的呼吸與嗅覺經驗出發，將閱讀、回憶與親歷地點之間的關係轉化為形式構想。在 [*Wasserlenken.*](https://yixincui.com/works/wasserlenken/) 中，古琴《神人暢》的旋律形態與同音高泛音的不同姿態，成為探索管弦樂音高、發聲方式與對位的材料。

其作品 [*Phalanx Break*](https://yixincui.com/works/phalanx-break/) 受 [Passacaglia Chamber Music Collective](https://westsidemusicfoundation.org/passacaglia/) 委約；[*Urtonforschungen II*](https://yixincui.com/works/urtonforschungen-ii/) 透過 [Wildflower Lab](https://wildflowercomposers.org/lab)，由 [Relâche Ensemble](https://www.relachemusic.org/) 排練並首演。《鼬ノ困》由 [Swarthmore College Orchestra](https://www.swarthmore.edu/music/swarthmore-college-orchestra) 與指揮 [Andrew Hauze](https://www.swarthmore.edu/profile/andrew-hauze-%E2%80%9904) 首演；*Twink Death* 透過 [Fetter Chamber Music Program](https://www.swarthmore.edu/music/fetter-chamber-music-program) 呈現首演。

崔浥新於 [Swarthmore College](https://www.swarthmore.edu/) 取得哲學學士學位，主要作曲老師為 [Gerald Levinson](https://geraldlevinson.com/about/)，現於 [Eastman School of Music](https://www.esm.rochester.edu/) 隨 [Daniel Pesca](https://www.esm.rochester.edu/directory/pesca-daniel/) 學習作曲。除創作外，亦從事聲樂、合作鍵盤演奏與指揮。

## English website version

Yixin Cui (b. 2002) is a composer based in Rochester, New York, writing orchestral, chamber, vocal, and music theatre works. Their practice also includes choreography combining Chinese Sign Language and percussion.

Language is a compositional material for Cui, who plays with phonetic borrowing, Japanese reading glosses, archaizing texts, and shifts of register to unsettle relationships between script, sound, and meaning. In [*Saijōtai Yamata[i]-K[h]itan No. 2*](https://yixincui.com/works/saijotai-yamatai-khitan-no-2/), the sounds of modern theoretical vocabulary, including libido and jouissance, are embedded in an archaizing text of Chinese characters and Japanese readings. In the music theatre work [*Twink Death*](https://yixincui.com/works/twink-death/), ancient characters speak through internet slang and theoretical discourse, while traditional music meets contemporary queer culture, queer phenomenology, and concepts of pornotopia and queertopia.

[*Impasse of the Weasels*](https://yixincui.com/works/impasse-of-the-weasels/), for orchestra, begins with experiences of breath and smell during a journey through Northeast China, translating relationships between reading, recollection, and encounters with a place into musical form. [*Wasserlenken.*](https://yixincui.com/works/wasserlenken/) draws on the melodic shapes of the guqin piece *Shen Ren Chang* and the differing gestures of guqin harmonics at a shared pitch, developing these materials through orchestral sound production and counterpoint.

[*Phalanx Break*](https://yixincui.com/works/phalanx-break/) was commissioned by the [Passacaglia Chamber Music Collective](https://westsidemusicfoundation.org/passacaglia/). [*Urtonforschungen II*](https://yixincui.com/works/urtonforschungen-ii/) was workshopped and premiered by [Relâche Ensemble](https://www.relachemusic.org/) through [Wildflower Lab](https://wildflowercomposers.org/lab). *Impasse of the Weasels* was premiered by the [Swarthmore College Orchestra](https://www.swarthmore.edu/music/swarthmore-college-orchestra), conducted by [Andrew Hauze](https://www.swarthmore.edu/profile/andrew-hauze-%E2%80%9904), and *Twink Death* received its premiere through the [Fetter Chamber Music Program](https://www.swarthmore.edu/music/fetter-chamber-music-program).

Cui holds a BA in Philosophy from [Swarthmore College](https://www.swarthmore.edu/), where their principal composition teacher was [Gerald Levinson](https://geraldlevinson.com/about/). They currently study composition with [Daniel Pesca](https://www.esm.rochester.edu/directory/pesca-daniel/) at the [Eastman School of Music](https://www.esm.rochester.edu/). Alongside composing, Cui is active as a singer, collaborative keyboardist, and conductor.

## Short English version

Yixin Cui (b. 2002) is a composer based in Rochester, New York, working across orchestral, chamber, vocal, and music theatre forms, as well as choreography using Chinese Sign Language. Their works explore memory, embodiment, and contemporary queer culture, with linguistic play through phonetic borrowing, archaizing texts, and shifts of register. Cui's music has been premiered by [Relâche Ensemble](https://www.relachemusic.org/) and the [Swarthmore College Orchestra](https://www.swarthmore.edu/music/swarthmore-college-orchestra), with a commission from the [Passacaglia Chamber Music Collective](https://westsidemusicfoundation.org/passacaglia/). A philosophy graduate of Swarthmore College, Cui studied composition primarily with [Gerald Levinson](https://geraldlevinson.com/about/) and currently studies with [Daniel Pesca](https://www.esm.rochester.edu/directory/pesca-daniel/) at the Eastman School of Music.
