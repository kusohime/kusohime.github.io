## Program Notes

On plateaus above <ruby><span class="ruby-base">3,000 meters</span><rt>~10,000 feet</rt></ruby>, spring does not officially arrive until July and often concludes hurriedly before September. Bayanbulak is the name of a pastoral expanse at these altitudes, where the sky is darker, the land is devoid of trees, and horses do not need reins as they gallop in parallel to cranes that skim over the green waves of grass.

## 節目說明

在海拔 <ruby><span class="ruby-base">3,000 公尺</span><rt>約一萬英尺</rt></ruby>以上的高原，春天要到七月才正式到來，又往往在九月之前便匆匆作結。<span class="mongolian-text" lang="mn-Mong">ᠪᠠᠶᠠᠨᠪᠤᠯᠠᠭ</span>，正是這樣一片高海拔牧野之名——那裡的天空更暗，大地不長樹木，馬兒無需韁繩，與掠過綠色草浪的鶴並肩奔馳。
