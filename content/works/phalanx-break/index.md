This piece is based on a melody allegedly from <ruby><span class="ruby-base">*[King Qin’s Breaking through the Enemy’s Phalanx](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Music_of_Prince_of_Qin_Breaking_up_the_Enemy%27s_Front)*</span><rt>秦王破陣樂</rt></ruby> (622) that was catalogued in<ruby><span class="ruby-base"> *[Sango-youroku](https://kokusho.nijl.ac.jp/biblio/100289785/1?ln=ja)*</span><rt>
三五要錄</rt></ruby> (~1192) for pipa. It is written for a [Nagahara Mini](https://www.nagaharaflutes.com/mini), a miniature flute.
